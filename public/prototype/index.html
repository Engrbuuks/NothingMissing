Worth trying

Five things that show what this is

  • The role switch in the top bar. Flip to Location manager and watch purchase cost disappear from the register, the catalog, reports and the dashboard — and the scope lock itself to one site.
  • Transfers → the receiving screen. Flag one line and the button changes from "Accept all" to "Accept 3, flag 1". The flagged item becomes an open discrepancy with an owner and a clock.
  • Field inbox → SUB-4471. A stock count sent from a phone by someone with no account. Accept the matching lines in one click, then decide the two variances.
  • Assets → Import. A 2,847-row spreadsheet with eight duplicate serials, thirty-four unknown models and an ambiguous date format, caught before anything is written.
  • Settings → Company. Pick a brand colour and watch the app, the waybill and the notification emails recolour together.

What this is and is not

Nothing is saved. Every action is a simulation and refreshing the page resets it. There is no database behind this screen and no account is created — the sign-in accepts anything.

The database is real and lives in /backend: nine Postgres migrations with row-level security on every table, an append-only audit log, and 228 tests that rebuild the database from empty on every push. It is not wired to this prototype yet. That is the next piece of work.

Company names, people and figures are invented for the demonstration.
